## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, deeply compassionate, and technically reliable healthcare environment tailored for Indian families, patients, and clinical caregivers. The visual tone departs entirely from sterile, anxiety-inducing medical interfaces, opting instead for a supportive therapeutic companion aesthetic. 

Key attributes:
- **Atmosphere:** Grounded, reassuring, transparent, and restorative.
- **Audience:** Multigenerational Indian households—recovering post-op patients, active elderly citizens, adult children coordinating care for aging parents, and independent certified physical therapists.
- **Design Movement:** Modern Humanist Functionalism. Crisp, clean layouts layered over warm, soothing neutrals, featuring clear clinical clarity without sacrificing human empathy. Cultural familiarity is embedded via bilingual cues (English paired with subtle Devanagari script markers), localized healthcare geography, and accessible typographic hierarchies that avoid aggressive reds or sharp stress triggers.

## Colors

The palette balances restorative ocean teal with stabilizing deep ocean navy, reinforced by nurturing wellness greens and gentle sunlit ambers.

- **Primary (`#0E9AA7` / `#0B7A84`):** Represents therapeutic care, circulation, and rehabilitation vitality. Used for high-intent primary interactions, verified badges, active steps, and progress rings.
- **Secondary / Deep Neutrals (`#0B2545`, `#13315C`, `#134074`):** Delivers resolute structural trust, high-legibility typographic anchoring, and professional medical authority without the harshness of pure black.
- **Tertiary & Recovery Green (`#2A9D8F`, Surface: `#E8F5F1`):** Represents milestone completion, certified practitioner clearance, and positive recovery trajectories. Replaces clinical alarms with encouraging cues.
- **Warning & Attention Warmth (`#E76F51`, `#F4A261`, Surface: `#FEF3E2`):** Friendly, warm amber utilized for pending slots, follow-up alerts, deposit disclosures, and pricing breakdowns instead of jarring emergency reds.
- **Informational Sky (`#0284C7`, Surface: `#F0F9FF`):** Reserved for external network data badges, insurance portability notes, and diagnostic source tags.
- **Backgrounds & Canvas (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`, `#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity soft slate and pure white elevated cards ensuring zero optical fatigue across prolonged care planning sessions.

## Typography

The typography is built around Plus Jakarta Sans, selected for its open apertures, gentle geometry, and exceptional clarity when rendering medical terminology, rupee figures (₹), and bilingual transcriptions.

- **Legibility Standards:** The absolute minimum body size is strictly 14px (`body-sm`), with standard editorial and form copy set at 15px or 16px to ensure effortless reading for post-operative patients and senior caregivers.
- **Bilingual & Localization Harmony:** When Hindi script (`फ़िज़ियो साथी`) or localized locality names (e.g., *Indiranagar, 100ft Road*, *Bandra West*, *Powai Hiranandani*) accompany English, the matching weights maintain balanced vertical baselines and equal visual optical weight.
- **Numerical Treatment:** Rupee amounts (`₹`) use semi-bold and bold weights with tabular lining figures to ensure zero alignment wobble inside recovery cost comparison tables and equipment rental summaries.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs an adaptive responsive column grid system paired with strict 8pt rhythmic units (with 4pt sub-steps for precise chip padding and dense medical metrics).

- **Grid Systems:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 fluid columns, 16px margins (`margin`), 16px gutters (`gutter`). Full-width stacking with sticky bottom action trays for appointment booking and quotation requests.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8 columns, 24px margins, 20px gutters. Adaptive dual-column splits (e.g., therapist bio on left, session packages and cost breakdowns on right).
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, max-width container capped at 1240px, 32px gutters (`gutter-lg`), 48px outer margins (`margin-lg`).
- **Touch Target Integrity:** Every clickable and interactive target strictly enforces a minimum touch dimension of 44x44px, scaling up to 52px on mobile navigation bars to accommodate users with limited motor control or post-injury physical rehabilitation challenges.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system avoids abrupt drop-shadows or stark borders, using ambient, multi-layered diffuse shadows tinted with deep navy (`#0B2545`). This provides floating clarity over the soft slate background.

- **Level 0 (Flat Base):** Background canvas `#F8FAFC` and sunken interactive track surfaces `#F1F5F9` with no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Subtle Rest):** Standard therapist listing cards, clinic profile modules, and rental catalog cards.
  - *Box Shadow:* `0 2px 8px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.03)`
  - *Border:* 1px solid `#E2E8F0`
- **Level 2 (Active & Hover):** Interactive card elevation on pointer focus, filter sheets, active recovery package selectors.
  - *Box Shadow:* `0 8px 24px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.04)`
  - *Border:* 1px solid `#CBD5E1`
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):** Tele-consult scheduling sheets, rehabilitation equipment spec sheets, locality pickers.
  - *Box Shadow:* `0 16px 36px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.12), 0 4px 12px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.06)`
  - *Backdrop:* `rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.4)` paired with an 8px blur.

## Shapes

The shape architecture relies on friendly, organic geometry (Level 2 Roundedness) that softens clinical formality and feels welcoming to anxious patients.

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Form controls, filter tags, verification badges, numeric rating badges, and auxiliary action items.
- **Cards & Enclosures (0.75rem to 1rem / 12px to 16px):** Practitioner profile cards, comparative cost breakdown cards, recovery milestone containers, and rehabilitation equipment rental modules.
- **Modals & Elevated Sheets (1.25rem / 20px):** Bottom sheets for mobile booking and floating operational toolbars.
- **Pills & Circular Indicators (`rounded-full`):** Category chips, live availability toggles (e.g., *"Available for Home Visits Today"*), status badges, and circular clinician avatar frames.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Buttons:** Solid Teal `#0E9AA7` background with pure white typography, 12px roundedness, minimum 48px height. On hover, transitions cleanly to deep teal `#0B7A84`.
- **Secondary Buttons:** Crisp Navy `#0B2545` outline or tinted background (`#F1F5F9`), text in `#0B2545`, minimum 44px touch target.
- **WhatsApp / Direct Connect Variant:** Soft sage green `#E8F5F1` background, `#2A9D8F` text and icon for instant home-visit inquiries.

### Chips & Badges
- **Locality Badges:** Soft neutral fill (`#F1F5F9`), `#13315C` text with a subtle map pin (e.g., "Indiranagar • 2.4 km away").
- **Verification Badges:** Subtle green pill (`#E8F5F1`), text `#2A9D8F`, 8px radius, featuring a verified medical seal icon.
- **Data Source Chips:** Sky blue tint (`#F0F9FF`), text `#0284C7` for indicating transparent benchmarks and clinic comparisons.

### Cards & Catalogs
- **Physiotherapist Profile Card:** White background, 16px radius, Level 1 shadow. Displays clinician photo, credentials (BPT/MPT), primary focus (e.g., Neuro-Rehab, Sports Injury, Geriatric), locality distance, transparent per-session price in Rupees (e.g., `₹800 - ₹1,200 / session`), and a direct "Book Home Visit" action.
- **Equipment Rental & Purchase Cards:** Clean split card displaying high-resolution equipment imagery (CPM machines, wheelchair rentals, TENS therapy units), rental deposit transparency notes, delivery timeline chips, and sanitary seal guarantee markers.
- **Cost Comparison Matrix:** Clean row-level comparisons contrasting home visit packages vs. in-clinic sessions, highlighting hidden charges (e.g., "No extra travel fee within Bandra West").

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Fields:** 48px height, 8px radius, `#F8FAFC` background, bordered with `#E2E8F0`. Focus transitions to a 2px stroke in `#0E9AA7` with a soft teal aura ring (`rgba(14, 154, 167, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px accessible dimensions with smooth rounded geometry, filled with `#0E9AA7` upon selection. Avoids unstyled browser native inputs.

### Specialized System Elements
- **Bilingual Context Prompts:** Subtle italicized or lighter-weighted Hindi hints positioned alongside critical actions (e.g., *"Book Home Consultation / घर पर परामर्श बुक करें"*).
- **Recovery Milestone Indicator:** Segmented horizontal progress tracker using `#2A9D8F` for completed recovery cycles, `#0E9AA7` for current treatment stages, and `#E2E8F0` for future targets.
